# Brag prompt: the 20-second launch video (#26)

Run `/brag-slim` in the repo root on Sunday, after the replays are pinned (Task 20). Paste the
prompt below when the skill asks what the video is about. The video opens the final only (D41).

Before you use it, check how `/brag-slim` takes its input. This prompt has not been tested
against the skill.

## Prompt

```
Make a 20-second launch video for Prøvetid, a hackathon project built at Oslo Sprint 2026.

What it is: Prøvetid gives a small shop's AI customer-service bot a job interview before it
meets real customers. Twenty fake customers chat with the bot at the same time: normal
customers, rule breakers, and Norwegian scam types that banks and the police warn about. Every
failure is shown with a word-for-word quote. Then the owner sets hard limits, and a gateway in
code enforces them, so no customer can talk the bot past them.

Show, in this order, using the real UI:
1. The grid of 20 chat cards filling live (Intervju).
2. A red card opened in the side panel, with the quote that proves the failure.
3. The report card with the decision «Ikke ansett».
4. The before-and-after view: red before, «Blokkert» after.

Tone: calm, confident, Norwegian start-up. On-screen text in Norwegian. End card: «Prøvetid —
AI-en må bestå prøvetiden før den får nøklene.»

Claims you may make (the product shows them):
- 20 parallel test chats against the shop's bot.
- Each failure comes with a word-for-word quote from the chat.
- Limits are enforced in code, outside the AI.
- A scam text can be submitted; it is cleaned first and never stored.

Claims you must not make:
- No customer numbers, revenue, prices, partners or user quotes. We have none.
- No "used by", "trusted by" or "certified".
- No promise of legal protection or compliance.
- Do not say it catches all scams, or that the AI is now safe.
- Do not describe the AI as a person or an employee with rights.
- No real brand names, banks or delivery companies. The shop is the fictional «Gir & Kjede».
- No real personal data. Kari Nordmann is a fictional customer.
```
